## Assessment

**In scope and substantive.** This is file 36924 / Directive (EU) 2023/2225 — not a `keep_apart` sibling.
A distinct event from prior TK issues #37 (WGO + moties), #35 (tweede nota van wijziging) and #39 (EK
referral). The genuinely new facts these documents establish:

1. **AFM scope interpretation published 20 Nov 2026** (the "normaal handelsverkeer" stappenplan,
   2026D60191) — the authoritative supervisor view of **when *uitstel van betaling* is credit**:
   - **Step 1 — continuous-service carve-out:** doorlopende dienstverlening / doorlopende levering of the
     same goods (gas/water/electricity, insurance, telecom, OV-/parking subscriptions and pay-after-use)
     is **not credit** (art 3(3) CCD2) — but an economic-substance test applies (one-off costs may not be
     "hidden" in a service subscription, e.g. solar panels / heat pump).
   - **Step 2 — platforms always in scope:** a platform (own + third-party products, new BW 7:57(1)(ab))
     offering deferred payment always provides credit; no exception applies.
   - **Steps 3–5 — the exception tiers:** physical shops + small/medium online suppliers may defer payment
     **≤50 days** with no interest/cost (only reasonable collection costs) and **no third party**; large
     online non-platform suppliers get a stricter **≤14 days** tier and additionally **may not sell the
     claim** (not even within the group).
   - **Factoring vs third-party BNPL boundary:** selling the receivable (factoring) does not make it credit;
     a BNPL third party at whom the *consumer* chooses the deferral does. AFM looks at the whole construction.
2. **Enforcement coulance period** — minister confirms AFM will apply a post-inwerkingtreding leniency in
   prioritising supervision (no full postponement), weighted by how foreseeable each norm was; addressed in
   the **AFM Agenda 2027** (Jan 2027).
3. **Entry into force now expected "begin 2027"** — the letter states inwerkingtreding "zal naar verwachting
   begin 2027 zijn", **slipping the 20 Nov 2026 application-date target** the repo currently records.
4. Fulfils moties Van Eijk/Schonis/Ceder (AFM scope richtlijnen) and Bushoff (age as risk factor in the
   leennormen); monitoring of 18–21 BNPL use (motie Ceder); €100-credit CCBR+BKR-only check in the
   Implementatiebesluit; **evaluation 3 yrs after entry into force (kabinetsreactie ~2030)**;
   **samenwerkingsprotocol incassodienstverlening** published in the Staatscourant.
