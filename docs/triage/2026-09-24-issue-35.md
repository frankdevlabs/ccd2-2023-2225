## Assessment

**Substantive and in scope.** The kamerstuk is file 36924 (this repo's `file_id`), not a `keep_apart`
sibling. The nota van wijziging (nr. 9) **amends the operative text** of the bill — seven items (A–G),
each implementing a specific CCD2 article:

- **A** — Definition of "krediet" (Wft 1:1): the continuous-service / continuous-delivery exception (CCD2
  art 3(3)) is added to limb **(a)** as well, not only limb (b) — broader effect.
- **B + C** — Cross-border / online credit: the bill's changes to **Wft 1:16** are dropped; a **new Wft
  1:16a** is inserted so Wft deel 1, 2 and 4 apply to (non-mortgage) consumer credit offered/brokered
  online into NL from another Member State within the directive's scope (art 2) — country-of-destination
  rule (lex specialis over the e-commerce directive; Rome I). Implements CCD2 art 37.
- **D + E** — **Registration regime for large (non-SME) brokers in goederenkrediet**: a **new Wft 2:81
  fifth lid** subjects large suppliers who broker goods credit as an ancillary activity to a *registration*
  duty (full exemption no longer allowed under CCD2 art 37(3)(a) for large enterprises — e.g. big telecom
  shops/webshops selling phones on credit); the existing 2:81(4) BNPL (≤3-months) registration group gets
  a "no advice" condition too; consequential renumbering + Wft 1:107(2)(a)(11°) update.
- **F** — Wft 4:34a: the ban on using **special-category / social-network data** in the creditworthiness
  test is **extended to the bemiddelaar** (broker). Implements CCD2 art 19(5).
- **G** — Wft 4:34c: extended to the bemiddelaar (borging of BW 7:58a). Implements CCD2 art 15.

The rapporteurs Q&A (Van Eijk/Ceder informatieverzoek 10 Sep → minister's reply 22 Sep) is a genuine
procedural step in the committee's preparation for the WGO. The **amendement-Flach (nr. 10)** on the credit
age-limit landed 24 Sep — recorded here as an emerging item; it is outside this issue's scope and, if it
proves durable, warrants its own flag/entry after the WGO.
